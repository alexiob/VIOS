## Findings for G1

1. Mode 0 fits: 36.9 KB internal free with pools, 12.9 KB above the floor.
2. Composition stays ahead of the beam even for a full screen at the measured
   blit rate, so beam-ordered single buffering works; the mode 129 fallback is
   not needed.
3. The VDP loop can be blocked for up to 66 ms by one VDU command: the ESP
   console's `show()` (VDU 23,0,E5,1) clears and queues ~4,600 fabgl glyph
   primitives while waiting on the primitive queue; a console flood E0 record
   costs up to 13 ms. A compositor started from the VDP loop would miss 3-4
   frames. G1 must (a) draw console/surface content with the direct blitter,
   not fabgl primitives (a full console redraw is ~6.5 ms at 719 cells/ms),
   (b) bound per-command work and split redraws across frames, and (c) start
   frames from the vertical-blank notification rather than a loop poll.
4. Free x costs ~2.2x per glyph; snap 8 as the default is right.
